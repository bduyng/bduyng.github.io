Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026
Developer: Duy Bao Nguyen (“we”, “us”)

Lines Blast is a color ball puzzle game. This policy explains what information the app uses, what stays on your device, and what is sent to the services that power the leaderboard and app analytics.

1. Summary

  • No sign-up, email, or password is needed to play.
  • Your best score, ball style, and settings are stored on your device.
  • The global leaderboard uses an anonymous player ID and a randomly generated name such as “Player 4821”.
  • We collect anonymous usage analytics to understand how the game is played and to fix crashes.
  • There are no ads and no advertising trackers.
  • The current version has no in-app purchases.

2. Stored on your device

  • Your best score
  • Your chosen ball style and theme
  • Your leaderboard name and anonymous player session
  • Your daily reminder preference

This data stays on your device unless you back up the device with Apple’s own backup tools. Uninstalling the app removes it.

3. Global leaderboard

To show the worldwide top scores, the app creates an anonymous player account with our backend provider, Supabase. It sends only:

  • A random anonymous user ID (not linked to your name, email, or Apple ID)
  • Your leaderboard name (randomly generated, e.g. “Player 4821”)
  • Your best score and when it was last updated

Leaderboard names and scores are visible to other players.

4. Analytics & diagnostics

We use PostHog to collect anonymous product analytics: which screens are opened, app open/close events, settings changes, and error reports. Events are tied to a random device identifier generated by the app, not to your identity. We do not use this data for advertising and do not sell it.

5. Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, Lines Blast schedules a local notification on your device. No push server is involved. You can turn it off in the app’s settings or in iOS Settings.

6. Microphone

The audio engine used for sound effects may show a microphone permission description. Lines Blast never records audio and does not use the microphone.

7. App updates

The app checks for updates through Expo Updates. These requests include technical information such as app version and platform so the right update can be delivered.

8. Children

Lines Blast is a general-audience game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Retention & deletion

  • Local data remains until you uninstall the app.
  • Leaderboard entries are kept while the leaderboard runs. Email us your leaderboard name to have your entry removed.
  • Analytics data is retained by PostHog under its standard retention policy.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date above will be revised when we do.
