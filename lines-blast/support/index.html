How to play

  • Tap a ball, then tap an empty cell it can reach to move it.
  • Line up 5 or more balls of the same color in a row, column, or diagonal to blast them.
  • Every move that doesn’t blast a line drops 3 new balls on the board.
  • Longer lines score more, and blasting several lines in one move multiplies the points.
  • The game ends when the board is full.

Leaderboard

Your best score is submitted automatically under a random name. The board needs an internet connection; the game itself works offline.
