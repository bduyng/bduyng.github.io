Doodle Sudoku

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 19, 2026
Developer: Duy Bao Nguyen (“we”, “us”)

Doodle Sudoku is a hand-drawn sudoku puzzle game. This policy explains what information the app uses, what stays on your device, and what is handled by Apple, Google, or our subscription provider when you buy Pro.

1. Summary

  • No account or sign-in is required to play.
  • Game progress, settings, and stats are stored on your device.
  • There are no ads and no third-party advertising trackers in the app.
  • Optional local reminders use your device’s notification system only when you turn them on.
  • If you subscribe to Pro, Apple or Google process payment; RevenueCat helps us verify your entitlement.

2. Information stored on your device

We store gameplay-related data locally on your phone or tablet, such as:

  • Active and saved puzzles, notes, mistakes, and timing
  • Daily challenge completion and streak history
  • Statistics (for example puzzles finished and best times)
  • Preferences (theme, board colors, sound, haptics, assist settings, notification toggle)
  • Local Pro entitlement cache after a purchase or restore

This data stays on your device unless you back up the device with your platform’s own backup tools. Uninstalling the app typically removes local app data.

3. Notifications

If you enable reminders, Doodle Sudoku schedules local notifications on your device (for example a daily play nudge). We do not use those reminders to send marketing push campaigns from our servers. You can turn notifications off in the app settings or in your device settings.

4. Purchases & subscriptions (Pro)

Pro is sold as an in-app subscription through the Apple App Store or Google Play. When you buy or restore Pro:

  • Apple or Google process your payment and provide a purchase receipt. We do not receive your full payment card number.
  • We use RevenueCat to validate receipts and unlock Pro features (unlimited hints, technique coaching, Smart Pencil, and assists). RevenueCat may process identifiers needed for purchase restoration (such as an anonymous app user ID and store transaction data).

See also RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy, Apple Privacy Policy, and Google Privacy Policy.

5. App updates

The app may check for updates (including Expo Updates / over-the-air updates when enabled). That process may involve standard technical network requests (such as device/app version information) so we can deliver a compatible update. Update hosting providers process those requests under their own terms.

6. What we do not collect

  • No email/password account system in the app
  • No advertising ID collection for ads (the app has no ads)
  • No analytics SDKs such as Firebase Analytics, Amplitude, Mixpanel, or similar product analytics were integrated at the time this policy was written
  • No sale of personal information

7. Children

Doodle Sudoku is a general-audience puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for marketing. Purchases follow the parental controls and payment rules of Apple or Google.

8. Data retention & deletion

  • Local gameplay data remains until you clear app data or uninstall.
  • Subscription records are retained by Apple/Google and RevenueCat as needed to provide the purchase and for legal/accounting obligations.
  • To cancel a subscription, use your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

9. Your choices

  • Play without creating an account
  • Keep notifications off (default until you enable them)
  • Decline or cancel Pro anytime via the store
  • Delete local data by uninstalling the app

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this page when the app’s privacy practices change. The “Effective date” above will be revised when we do. Continued use of the app after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.
